## PARKED — Sequencer Timed Words
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Allow individual phases in a sequence to trigger a specific word or short
phrase at a precise time offset within the phase. The timed word overrides
whatever the regular text overlay is currently displaying for a set duration,
with its own independent styling (color, size, animation, flash) before
handing control back to the normal phrase cycle.

Open design questions before implementing:
- Timing anchor: phase-relative offset vs. absolute sequence timestamp?
- Multiple timed words per phase?
- Should timed words fire during the transition window or only during the hold?
- Own styling per entry, or inherit from the phase?
- Own flash/strobe settings independent of the phase's flash settings?
- When the word expires, does the regular phrase cycle resume mid-rotation
  or restart from the beginning?
- Behaviour when the word overlaps a phase boundary (cut off vs. let finish)?
- Behaviour on pause/resume if the trigger time has already passed?

## PITCHED — Aggressive / Disorienting Features (brief)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Subliminal Single-Frame Text Bursts
Flash text for just 1–2 frames (16–33 ms at 60 fps) — short enough that
the conscious mind does not fully register a readable word, but long enough
that the visual cortex processes it. Operates completely independently of
the regular text overlay cycle. Each burst has its own trigger interval,
word list, color, and size. A sequencer phase could ramp the burst rate
from sparse to relentless as depth increases.
